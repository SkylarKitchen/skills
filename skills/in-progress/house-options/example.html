    How the rough cost was made

    Rough construction cost rates: national averages for a custom-built house, in 2026 dollars, multiplied by measured quantities and a location factor. A planning figure for comparing options, never a quote or a bid. lib.js costOf() reads this file; DATA.md explains each line. Each plan's quantities (floor, wall, roof and glass areas, baths, stairs, porch) are measured from its drawings and multiplied by the national-average rates below. The total is then adjusted for the region, and a builder's overhead and a contingency are added. Rates as of 2026-10-28.

    Location factor = 1 + 0.45 x (state mean hourly wage / US mean - 1), with 0.45 assumed as labour's share of house construction cost. Wages: BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025, construction and extraction occupations (SOC 47-0000), mean hourly wage; US $31.42 (series OEUN000000000000047000003 and OEUS<state>0000000000047000003, fetched 2026-10-28). Alaska and Hawaii add 0.15 for shipped materials (assumption).

    What the drawings assume

    1. Sun angles are for noon at 40.2° N: 26° above the horizon on 21 December, 73° on 21 June. The section is cut square to the house, so on a house turned on its lot the rays show the angle the sun makes in that cut.
    2. A slab floor stands 8 in above the ground, a crawlspace or pier floor 30 in.
    3. Site facts given: lot, setbacks, arrival, views. Nothing else about the site is shown as fact.
    4. Each wish marked yes or no is measured from the plan data. A wish that cannot be measured is judged by the graders.
