The options

How to read a sheet

Each option has one sheet. At the top, the diagrams show how the form was made, step by step, with the main move in red. Then the plans (north arrow turned to the true north on this lot), a section cut through the house with the noon sun at midwinter and midsummer, a view from above, and a site plan with true north up. Under them: the graders' scores, each wish measured from the plan, and the rough cost.

Still to draw

    What the drawings assume

    1. Sun angles are for noon at {{LAT}}° N: {{SUN_DEC}}° above the horizon on 21 December, {{SUN_JUN}}° on 21 June. The section is cut square to the house, so on a house turned on its lot the rays show the angle the sun makes in that cut.
    2. A slab floor stands 8 in above the ground, a crawlspace or pier floor 30 in.
    3. Site facts given: {{KNOWN}}. Nothing else about the site is shown as fact.
    4. Each wish marked yes or no is measured from the plan data. A wish that cannot be measured is judged by the graders.
