# Registry: {{FILL: how many}} plan types for {{FILL: the house's title}}

Each entry is one plan type that no other entry uses. The headline move is what makes the entry unique; keep it legible in the plan, the axon and the diagrams. Sizes are guides, not rules. The precedents are the ones to cite first, from the research notes in `sources/`; others from the notes may be added if they are really used.

Each entry's number, name and one plain line also go wherever the page reads the registry (DATA.md says where; build.sh refuses a mismatch). Entries are drawn four at a time, one batch per heading.

Every entry gives:
- **the move**: two to four sentences on the rooms and where they sit, rough size, storeys and the roof;
- **on the lot**: how the house is turned and where it sits inside the setbacks;
- **foundation**: slab, crawl or piers, if the brief leaves it open;
- **wishes**: the ids of the wishes it leans on;
- **precedents**: two to four from the notes, each with what it lends.

An entry for the form (the precedent names are placeholders):

> **07 The ell.** An east–west living bar with a short bedroom wing turned south at the east end (the wing no longer than about 1.3 × its width), and a screened room in the corner between them, facing south-west. About 1,900 sf, one storey, one gable on the bar and a lower gable on the wing. On the lot: turned 10° east of south, near the north setback so the drive is short. Foundation: slab. Wishes: w1, w3. Precedents: Example House A (architect: a bar with a short bedroom wing and a screened porch), Example House B (architect: a big share of the house as screened porch).
